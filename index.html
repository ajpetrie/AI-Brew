01 / EXECUTIVE SNAPSHOT

Executive Snapshot

This week’s defining shift is from AI assistants toward systems that can take action across enterprise software, devices, and physical operations. Apple is opening Siri capabilities to third-party apps; OpenAI and ServiceNow are connecting models and agents to real workflows; and Google is pushing its Gemini 4 family into cybersecurity and long-horizon engineering. Meanwhile, Anthropic’s amended IPO filing puts the economics of frontier AI—revenue growth, losses, and extraordinary compute commitments—under a public-market spotlight.

02 / HALLWAY TAKEAWAY

Hallway Takeaway

The competitive moat is increasingly the operating layer around the model: distribution, integrations, identity, permissions, inference economics, and trusted execution. The question for executives is no longer simply “Which model is best?” It is “Which system can safely complete a measurable business process, with the right human controls and a sustainable cost structure?”

03 / THE AGENT ECONOMY

The agent economy needs an access layer

As agents move from answering questions to changing records, writing code, and coordinating work, organizations need a control plane: scoped identities, least-privilege permissions, approval thresholds, audit trails, rollback paths, and clear accountability. Treat every agent as a digital worker with a defined job—not as a super-user.

11 / AI QUESTION OF THE WEEK

What should an AI agent be allowed to do without asking you?

If an AI agent could access your email, calendar, CRM, and financial systems, which actions would you allow it to complete autonomously—and which should always require your approval? Consider the difference between reading, drafting, recommending, and executing.

12 / AI TIP

Give your agent a “stop and ask” rule

When building or configuring an agent, define an explicit approval boundary. Example: “You may research, summarize, and draft independently. Before sending messages, changing customer records, spending money, sharing sensitive data, or deleting anything, stop and ask for my approval.”
